Questions

Is the YouTube stream really free — no catch?

Yes. Sports Central is the PCB's streaming partner and carries this series free on YouTube for viewers in regions where the paid apps hold no exclusive rights. You may see normal YouTube ads, but there is no subscription, no package and no payment screen. If YouTube ever shows the stream as blocked in your country, PTV Sports on TV and the free live scores above still cost nothing.

How do I watch free on TV?

Turn on PTV Sports — Pakistan's free-to-air sports channel, available on a normal TV antenna and basic cable. There is nothing to install and nothing to pay. For today's match online, the free Cricbuzz scorecard linked above has every ball — the Sports Central YouTube stream only plays outside Pakistan.

What time do the remaining matches start?

Every match in this tri-series starts at 1:00 PM Pakistan time, with the toss at 12:30 PM: Pakistan vs Sri Lanka on Tue 27 Oct, Sri Lanka vs England on Thu 29 Oct, and the final on Sat 31 Oct — all at Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore.

Can I watch other countries' cricket here too — India, Australia, England…?

Yes — the Cricket From Every Country section above links all twelve major teams' official board channels: Pakistan, India, Australia, England, South Africa, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, West Indies, Afghanistan, Ireland and Zimbabwe. They're all free: boards stream their domestic, women's and A-team matches live on them and post international highlights. Live internationals themselves are usually on paid TV in most countries — when a board or the ICC streams one free, it's on that country's channel or ICC.tv.

Where do I watch the next tournament for free?

The pattern is the same almost every series: check the PCB and Sports Central YouTube channels first for free streams and highlights, ICC.tv for ICC events (World Cups, Champions Trophy) in regions without a TV deal, and PTV Sports on TV. This page is rebuilt for each big series with the working free links.

Can I install Live Cricket as an app on my phone?

Yes — two ways, both free. On Android, tap Download APK in the Get the App section above: the real app file (13 KB, Android 8+) downloads straight to your phone; open it, tap Install, and the Live Cricket icon lands on your home screen. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser — that's the normal one-time step for any APK downloaded outside the Play Store. Prefer not to download a file? The same section's “install from the browser” button puts Live Cricket on your home screen without any file. On iPhone there is no APK — Apple only allows installing from Safari's Share button → “Add to Home Screen”. Either way the app opens this page full-screen from its own icon, and whenever the page updates, the app shows the newest version.

Why not just list the pirate streaming sites?

Those sites get shut down mid-match, bury the video under pop-ups, and some push risky downloads. Every link here is an official channel, so the stream stays up for the whole match — and it never asks for your card or a package.
